Questions

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to core questions regarding our collaboration and legal services.

How can I schedule an initial consultation?

You can contact us by phone or email to arrange an appointment at our office or via video conference (Zoom/Teams), depending on what suits you best.

What documents should I bring to the first meeting?

For the best and fastest service, it is helpful to bring your ID card, your Tax Identification Number (TIN), as well as any documents directly related to your case (e.g., contracts, court decisions, formal notices, correspondence, or public documents).

How much does the initial consultation cost?

The fee for the initial consultation depends on the complexity of the case. In any case, during our first communication, you will be fully and transparently informed about the cost of the consultation or file review, with no hidden charges.

How is the fee for handling a case determined?

The fee depends on the complexity, the nature of the case, and the required time investment. In our firm, we follow a policy of absolute transparency: you are informed in detail about the costs and alternative options from the very first meeting, before we take any action.

Do you handle cases outside the area of your office’s headquarters?

Yes, our firm has an extensive network of associates throughout Greece and can handle and execute cases outside our primary headquarters, whether regarding court appearances or out-of-court actions.

Is it mandatory for a case to go to court?

Not always. Our firm's primary goal is to exhaust every possibility for an out-of-court settlement or mediation, as this saves time and money for the client. We resort to litigation only when the judicial route is the sole or optimal solution for your interests.

How can I stay updated on the progress of my case?

Our commitment is to provide regular and responsible updates to our clients. For every significant development (filing of legal pleadings, scheduling of a court date, issuance of a decision), we contact you immediately via email or phone. You can also schedule an update call with the attorney handling your file.
