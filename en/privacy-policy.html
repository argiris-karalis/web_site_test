Privacy Policy

The protection of your personal data is of paramount importance to our law firm. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website.

1. Data Collection

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us through phone or email (such as Full Name, Email, Phone Number, and your message), solely for the purpose of responding to your requests.

2. Use of Information

Your data is used exclusively to communicate with you and to provide the legal services you entrust to us. We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement modern technical and organizational security measures, including SSL encryption, to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, rectify, restrict, or completely delete your personal data from our records at any time. You may contact us regarding any such request.
